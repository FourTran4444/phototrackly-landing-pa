# Resource editorial policy

These four guides are practical operating templates, not customer case studies or claims about released PhotoTrackly features. They are written for US and Australian property media teams. Before publishing further guides:

- Use real workflow questions from consented discovery conversations when possible. Remove identifying property and client details.
- Keep examples labeled as examples; do not invent survey results, customer quotes or measured savings.
- Review each guide for accurate scope and a useful next action. Update an existing guide when a question fits rather than generating near-duplicate pages.
- Link each guide to the relevant planned product page and to the early-access form. Record substantive updates with a real date.
- Inspect impressions, useful search queries, and confirmed lead registrations monthly. Bing AI citations can supplement this review; they do not guarantee leads or coverage.
